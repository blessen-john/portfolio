How I would approach it

Three hypothetical scenarios, not client work. They show how I would plan delivery on the platforms above.

Hypothetical scenario

Adobe Experience Platform for a multi-language B2B manufacturer

  1. Map the data sources (website, forms, CRM, Marketo Engage) and agree the first use cases with the business.
  2. Design the XDM schemas and identity rules so web, lead and account data resolve to one profile.
  3. Connect sources and web data collection, with consent and data-usage labels in place from day one.
  4. Build audiences and activate them in Marketo Engage and paid media.
  5. Hand over with naming standards, access roles, documentation and team onboarding.

Hypothetical scenario

Marketing automation: a lead lifecycle in Marketo Engage

  1. Agree lifecycle stages and the definition of a sales-ready lead with sales.
  2. Build a scoring model that combines behaviour and fit, with score decay.
  3. Set up nurture programmes by product interest and language.
  4. Automate the sales handoff with alerts, CRM sync and follow-up tracking.
  5. Report the funnel in Looker Studio and review it monthly with sales and marketing.

Hypothetical scenario

A global content supply chain on Workfront and AEM

  1. Replace email requests with one Workfront intake form and routing rules.
  2. Use Workfront Fusion to turn approved requests into tasks and notify the right people.
  3. Author once in the AEM language master, translate by machine, then review locally.
  4. Approve and roll out to live copies through a single publishing workflow.
  5. Track turnaround and workload in dashboards, and adjust the process each quarter.
